Our Story

A family business, built on doorsteps.

We started All Star Carpet Care in 1996 with one van, one phone line, and a simple promise: do the work right, charge what's fair, and treat every customer the way we'd want to be treated.

Three decades later, that promise hasn't changed. We still answer our own phones. We still show up when we say we will. And we still clean carpets the way they ought to be cleaned — thoroughly, with care, and with the kind of attention you only get from people who own the company they work for.

Thirty years. Same family. Same standards. — The All Star Carpet Care Team
